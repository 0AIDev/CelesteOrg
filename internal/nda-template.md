## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" shall mean any and all non-public information, whether written, oral, electronic, or visual, disclosed by the Company to the Authorized Party, including but not limited to:

- **(a)** Source code, algorithms, data models, system architectures, APIs, and technical documentation;
- **(b)** Business plans, strategies, financial information, pricing models, and revenue projections;
- **(c)** Customer and user data, usage patterns, analytics, and behavioral insights;
- **(d)** Product roadmaps, feature specifications, design mockups, and prototype materials;
- **(e)** Employee information, organizational structures, and compensation details;
- **(f)** Marketing strategies, partnership agreements, and vendor contracts;
- **(g)** Trade secrets, inventions, discoveries, and know-how;
- **(h)** Any information marked as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with similar designation;
- **(i)** Information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

### 1.2 Intellectual Property

"Intellectual Property" shall mean all creations, inventions, improvements, and works of authorship conceived, developed, or reduced to practice by the Authorized Party during the term of their engagement with the Company, whether or not patentable or copyrightable.

### 1.3 Company Materials

"Company Materials" shall mean all equipment, devices, software, documents, data, and other property provided by the Company or created using Company resources.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

### 2.1 Non-Disclosure

The Authorized Party agrees to:

- **(a)** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
- **(b)** Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent of the Company;
- **(c)** Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing their duties for the Company;
- **(d)** Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure, including compliance with the Company's information security policies;
- **(e)** Immediately notify the Company of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

### 2.2 Standard of Care

The Authorized Party shall protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care they use to protect their own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

### 2.3 Permitted Disclosures

The obligations under this Article shall not apply to information that:

- **(a)** Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Authorized Party;
- **(b)** Was rightfully known to the Authorized Party prior to disclosure;
- **(c)** Is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction on disclosure;
- **(d)** Is independently developed by the Authorized Party without use of Confidential Information;
- **(e)** Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the Authorized Party gives prompt written notice to the Company.

## ARTICLE 3 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

### 3.1 Work Product

The Authorized Party hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to any and all Intellectual Property conceived, developed, or created during the term of their engagement, including but not limited to:

- **(a)** Software, code, algorithms, and technical implementations;
- **(b)** Documentation, specifications, and design materials;
- **(c)** inventions, whether or not patentable;
- **(d)** Creative works, including text, graphics, and multimedia content;
- **(e)** Processes, methods, and workflows.

### 3.2 Prior Inventions

The Authorized Party has listed on **Exhibit A** (attached hereto) all inventions, discoveries, and works of authorship made prior to this Agreement that the Authorized Party wishes to exclude from the scope of this assignment ("Prior Inventions"). If no Prior Inventions are listed, the Authorized Party represents that no such Prior Inventions exist.

### 3.3 Moral Rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Authorized Party waives any moral rights in the Intellectual Property assigned hereunder, including the right of attribution and the right to object to modifications.

### 3.4 Cooperation

The Authorized Party agrees to execute any documents and take any actions reasonably necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of the assigned Intellectual Property, including patent and copyright applications.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — RETURN OF MATERIALS

### 4.1 Upon Termination

Upon termination of the engagement, or upon the Company's request, the Authorized Party shall:

- **(a)** Immediately return all Company Materials and Confidential Information;
- **(b)** Permanently delete all copies of Confidential Information from personal devices and storage;
- **(c)** Provide written certification of deletion upon request;
- **(d)** Not retain any copies, excerpts, or summaries of Confidential Information.

### 4.2 Exceptions

The Authorized Party may retain copies of Confidential Information solely to the extent required by applicable law or professional standards, provided such copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — NON-COMPETITION & NON-SOLICITATION

### 5.1 Non-Competition

During the term of engagement and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Authorized Party shall not directly or indirectly engage in, or have any ownership interest in, or participate in the financing, operation, management, or control of any business that competes with the Company's current or planned products or services.

### 5.2 Non-Solicitation

During the term of engagement and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Authorized Party shall not:

- **(a)** Solicit or recruit any employee, contractor, or consultant of the Company;
- **(b)** Encourage any such person to leave the Company's service;
- **(c)** Solicit or attempt to solicit any customer, client, or business partner of the Company for the purpose of providing competing products or services.

The Authorized Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 6.2 Indemnification

The Authorized Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses arising from any breach of this Agreement.

### 6.3 Attorney's Fees

In the event of any legal action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

## ARTICLE 7 — TERM AND TERMINATION

### 7.1 Term

### 7.2 Survival
